Chat Faces 1.1.0

Minecraft 1.21.1, Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.22 or newer in the 21.1 series.
Install Figura 0.1.6+1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Chat-Faces-1.1.0.jar in the client's
mods folder. Remove the older Chat Faces JAR. Chat Heads is not required; remove
it to avoid duplicate portraits. Restart Minecraft and enable Figura portraits.

Normal death messages carry information identifying the attacker. Servers that
replace them with plain text may remove the identity needed to show a portrait.
